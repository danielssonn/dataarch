Data Platform: Executive Overview

Vision

Build a greenfield, Azure-native, event-driven data platform whose foundation is a bank-grade ontology and taxonomy. This platform will:

  • Launch client statements as the first production-grade, near-real-time capability (Q2 2027).
  • Provide a governed landing zone for supply chain integrations, letters of credit, and lending data (starting Q4 2027).
  • Serve as the single authoritative source for transactional and relational data across products and geographies.
  • Support near-real-time consumer/provider integrations (internal systems, workflows, AI) without tight coupling.

Strategic Rationale

  • Today: fragmented data, point-to-point integrations, no unified model.
  • Tomorrow: a coherent platform that is:
    • Ontology-first: all models, pipelines, and APIs derive from a central taxonomy.
    • Event-driven: real-time decision and reporting backbone via Kafka and streaming.
    • Governed: strong security, lineage, PCI DSS 4.x alignment from day one.
    • Scalable: architecture designed for multi-year growth with 10 engineers and lean operations.

Core Design Decisions

  • Cloud: Azure (confirmed).
  • Lakehouse: ADLS Gen2 + Delta Lake + Databricks.
  • Streaming/Messaging: Confluent Kafka on Azure (approved).
  • Services: AKS for APIs, Flink/streaming apps, and future workflow orchestrators.
  • Graph/Relationships: Neo4j (recommended) aligned to ontology and Delta Lake.
  • Governance: Unity Catalog (via Databricks) for catalog, lineage, access control.
  • Security: PCI DSS 4.x controls baked in (segmentation, encryption, audit, least privilege).

Phased Roadmap (High-Level)

  • 0–90 days: Foundation
    • Ontology implemented and enforced.
    • Secure Azure landing zone with Databricks, Confluent Kafka, AKS.
    • Batch and streaming pipelines from ledger and payments to canonical models.
    • Flink-based streaming foundation for statement generation.
    • Statement engine prototype on synthetic/early data.
  • Q2 2027: MVP – Client Statements
    • Hardened, production-grade statement generation pipelines and APIs.
    • Near-real-time accuracy, auditability, PCI-compliant protections.
  • Q4 2027: Supply Chain, LCs, Lending
    • Extended ontology to support LCs, lending, vendor integrations.
    • Containment zones for external/vendor data.
    • New canonical models and streaming flows for trade finance and lending.
  • 2028–2030: Advanced Capabilities
    • AI/ML on top of canonical data.
    • Self-service analytics, data products, and policy-as-code governance.

What We Are Asking For

  • Approval for:
    • This architecture and stack (Azure, Databricks, Confluent Kafka, AKS, Neo4j, Unity Catalog).
    • Treating the data ontology as a non-negotiable governance artifact.
    • Using the 90-day plan as the foundation for the next 12–24 months of investment.
  • Commitment:
    • 10 data engineers allocated.
    • Architecture alignment across business and IT teams.
    • No rogue pipelines or shadow data stores that bypass the platform.
