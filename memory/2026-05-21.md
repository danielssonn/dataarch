#### Critical Architectural Tenet Captured — Intent-First Ontology (#2)
Top tier of ontology = CLIENT INTENT, not technical implementation. What does corporation need? What can bank sell?
```
Client Intent → Product/Service Offering → Technical Rail Selection (auto-resolved) → Domain Execution
```
Maya never sees "Fedwire" or "Lynx." She says "send $5M to Canada subsidiary." Platform resolves: CAD→Lynx. Ontology records both but keeps abstraction levels separate.
Correlation maintained end-to-end for full auditability without forcing technical details upward into business semantics.

**This tenet is critical and guides all future design decisions.**
- Geography + rail availability = operational constraints, NOT ontology concepts themselves (they're metadata on the technical layer beneath client-facing abstractions)
- ISO 20022 message-level data lives in Containment zone initially; progressive mapping lifts signal upward through rail→product→intent layers over time
