# 2026-05-20 — Global Transaction Banking Platform Discovery

## Engagement: Initial Requirements Gathering (Telegram DM)

### Business Context Captured
- **Client:** Top 10 North American bank, Global Transaction Banking division
- **Driver:** Growth + market penetration
- **Scope:** Mix of greenfield build and legacy data silos to integrate
- **Target customers:** Fortune 50-scale corporates as design blueprint (if it works for them, works for all)

### Product Lines
1. Cash Management
2. Liquidity
3. Trade Finance
4. Supply Chain Finance

Most core offerings are vendor-hosted systems — not homegrown applications.

### Scale Assumptions
- **Year 1:** ~200 corporate customers → growing to ~1,000 by Year 5
- **Transaction volumes:** Low to medium (not high-frequency trading scale)

### Platform & Cloud
- **Core platform:** Databricks — Daniel will stand up and own the environment
- **Cloud:** Azure
- **Integration pattern:** APIs / microservices for vendor system connectivity; keep loosely coupled

### Solution Tenets
1. **Bi-directional streaming** — data flows INTO and OUT of the platform (not one-way ingestion)
2. **Near-real-time data exposure** to diverse consumers including internal systems AND external clients
3. This is NOT a pure analytics play — operational serving is first-class alongside analytical workloads

### Key Architectural Decisions
- **Ontology-first approach.** Heavy reliance on sound ontology model as foundational layer for cross-product-line semantic consistency
- **Two distinct artifacts planned:**
  - Data Ontology Model (standalone)
  - Data Exposure Patterns (separate artifact, but informed by the ontology)
